The Pain of Cloud-Dependent Creative Tools

Every designer and developer has experienced lost work due to dropped connections, slow asset uploads to distant servers, or surprise subscription paywalls for basic CSS export. Looply runs 100% in your browser using Dexie.js and IndexedDB.

By operating directly against your browser's persistent client storage, operations execute in less than 5 milliseconds without network latency, rate limits, or account requirements.

Zero Data Leakage for Unreleased Brand Assets

When working on confidential client redesigns or unreleased product screenshots, uploading assets to third-party servers presents a security concern. With Looply, your images and project data never leave your local hardware.

  • 🔒 100% Local Storage: Assets are stored as Data URLs and Blob records in IndexedDB.
  • ⚡ Offline Ready: Works seamlessly on airplanes, trains, or low-connectivity environments.
  • 📦 Universal Export: Export self-contained CSS or full ZIP bundles with one click.

Instant Compilation Without Servers

Transforming keyframe matrices into production CSS and ZIP bundles happens directly in the browser thread via JSZip and pure string templates, yielding sub-millisecond exports with zero carbon footprint.
