The Geometry of Cubic-Bézier Curves

A cubic-bézier curve is defined by four coordinates: P0(0,0), P1(x1, y1), P2(x2, y2), and P3(1,1). The tangent vector at P0 sets the initial acceleration, while the tangent at P3 dictates how the element settles into its rest position.

Modern Snappy Entrance

cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

High initial speed with prolonged deceleration. Ideal for modal dialogs and dropdown menus.

Punchy Dismissal / Exit

cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)

Zero hesitation at the departure point. Elements quickly leave the screen without lingering.
